08 / BEAST ATTACK · TLS 1.0 CBC

A strong cipher. A predictable IV.

BEAST (2011): Browser Exploit Against SSL/TLS. TLS stands for Transport Layer Security. TLS 1.0 used CBC with a predictable next IV.

01 / 04
Historical protocol demonstration. No browser exploit or real cookie extraction occurs here.
STORY IN FOUR MOVES

DON'T CONFUSE THESE ATTACKS

BEAST: predictable CBC IV + chosen plaintext in TLS 1.0.
Padding oracle: a server reveals whether the decrypted padding is valid.
Lucky Thirteen: timing leakage in CBC record processing.

Fixes include safer protocol design and modern authenticated encryption such as AES-GCM. TLS 1.3 does not use CBC cipher suites.
