BLOCK CIPHERS / SLIDE 01 / THE WHY

Why Confusion & Diffusion?

Two different problems. Two complementary design principles.

01 / HIDE RELATIONSHIPS

Confusion

Can we infer the secret key from the ciphertext?

Make the link between key and ciphertext difficult to analyze.

02 / SPREAD INFLUENCE

Diffusion

What if we change just one input bit?

Spread small input changes across many output positions.

Design goal Conceal relationships and spread influence. Neither principle is enough alone.
